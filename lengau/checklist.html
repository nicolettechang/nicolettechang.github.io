1. Err, wait... what's happening?

  • CHPC shuts Lengau down on 1 December 2026 and deletes everything on it, your home folder and your scratch included.
  • Really? Yes, they said so.
  • Nothing moves to the new system. It is due in March 2027, with the date not yet confirmed.
  • Move your data off by Friday 27 November, CHPC's deadline. The countdown shows how long is left.
  • You can run jobs until CHPC's job cut-off, which they will announce. But you still need time to copy your data off before 1 December when all jobs still running will be killed.
  • Anything you need between December and March must be on your laptop or your drive.

2. What do you need?

  • Your laptop. Hopefully with some space available on it.
  • CHPC access. Connection to network through CHPC VPN.
  • An external drive with enough space for your data and a bit more. If it is a new drive, format it exFAT (Mac and Windows) or APFS (Mac only), and not FAT32: it cannot hold files over 4 GB, and model files are bigger. Disk Utility → select the drive → Erase → ExFAT. Erasing deletes everything on the drive.
  • Time. Over the VPN a copy runs at about 30 GB an hour. 100 GB takes an afternoon, 1 TB a day and a half. Sort before you copy.
  • This guide. Keep following.

How this guide works

  • This guide will provide a suggested workflow to help you get the necessary data off Lengau.
  • The terminal commands are also provided. Customise them for your username in the box below.
  • A download list to track the folders for download is provided. This will write both the commands or the script to help you download your data. You can keep it open in another browser tab or use the cart button at the top right of every page.
