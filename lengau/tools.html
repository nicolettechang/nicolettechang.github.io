Snapshot viewer

Made a snapshot with the faster method on the Choose page? Open it here to see how much space you are using, with the largest folders first. Nothing to tick: to pick folders to keep, use your download list.

A snapshot of every folder shows each folder's size and how many files are in it. A snapshot of every file also shows the 200 largest files in each folder.

Nothing is uploaded. Your snapshot is read in this browser and forgotten when you close the page.
